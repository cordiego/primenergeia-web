Privacy Policy (Aviso de Privacidad)

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

At PRIMEnergeia S.A.S. ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy is aligned with the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares, or LFPDPPP) of Mexico, as well as international data protection standards.

1. Data Collection

We may collect personal information that you provide to us directly when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, or otherwise contact us. This may include your name, email address, and company affiliation.

2. Use of Information

We use the information we collect to communicate with you, provide requested services or information, improve our offerings, and for security and legal compliance purposes.

3. Data Protection and ARCO Rights

In accordance with the LFPDPPP, you have the right to Access, Rectify, Cancel, or Oppose (ARCO rights) the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@primenergeia.com.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, or wish to exercise your ARCO rights, please contact us.
